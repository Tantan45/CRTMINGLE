Terms of Service

CRTMINGLE is a text-only student chat for the College for Research and Technology. CRT staff should review these terms before student use.

Access

Clicking Start Chatting creates an anonymous session. Visitors confirm in the Rules popup that they are CRT students. No personal account, login, email, or student password is used. Chat names are optional; blank names appear as “Stranger.”

Expected behavior

Be respectful. Do not threaten, harass, bully, send sexual content, or share contact details or other personal information. Do not try to evade a block or moderation action. Text messages are filtered and may be replaced with [removed].

Moderation

Chat messages are logged for up to 30 days. Students can report or block a chat partner. CRT moderators can review chat context and ban a session or hashed fingerprint. Repeated reports can trigger a temporary suspension.

Availability and support

Chat access follows hours set by CRT administrators. This is not an emergency service. Contact CRT student services or use the support information on the Safety Guidelines page.
