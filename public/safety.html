STUDENT SAFETY

Keep it kind. Keep it safe.

CRTMINGLE is a text-only chat for CRT students. Start chatting to create an anonymous session, then confirm in the Rules popup that you are a CRT student. You may stop, find someone new, block, or report at any time.

Protect your information

  • Do not share your phone number, home address, school schedule, financial details, social handles, or other personal information.
  • Do not send sexual content, threats, harassment, hateful language, or bullying.
  • Automated filters remove many contact details and unsafe terms, but filters can miss context. Report a concern to a moderator.

Reports and chat records

Chat messages are retained for up to 30 days. A report gives moderators the last 20 messages from that room, the anonymous session ID, and the hashed device/IP fingerprint. Three reports within the configured period can suspend a session and fingerprint.

Need support?

Contact your school counselor through CRT student services.
