Privacy Policy

CRTMINGLE does not create personal accounts and does not ask for a name, email address, or student password. Clicking Start Chatting creates an anonymous session; the Rules popup asks visitors to confirm they are CRT students.

What the service logs

  • An anonymous session ID stored in a signed, secure session cookie.
  • A keyed hash of the visitor IP address, browser user-agent, and random browser token, used to prevent repeat matching after a block or ban. The raw IP address and token are not stored by the app.
  • Chat messages, sender session ID, room ID, and timestamps for safety moderation.
  • Reports, report reason, the last 20 messages from the reported chat, block relationships, session and fingerprint bans, and staff audit actions.

Who can see the logs

Only staff authorized for the protected CRTMINGLE Admin Portal can review reports, chat context, session IDs, hashed fingerprints, bans, and audit records. Chat partners see only an optional nickname or “Stranger.”

How long data is kept

Chat messages, room records, reports, warnings, and staff audit records are automatically deleted after 30 days. Inactive anonymous sessions are deleted after 30 days. A hashed fingerprint attached to an active ban is kept until the ban expires so clearing a cookie does not immediately bypass it.

Security and contact

The app uses an HTTP-only, same-site session cookie and marks it secure when served over HTTPS. CRT should protect the database, backups, and staff access. For questions, contact CRT student services.
