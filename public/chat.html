A FRIENDLY REMINDER

Keep it kind. Keep it safe.

CRTMINGLE is for College for Research and Technology students. You confirm this before chatting. No account is collected. Your anonymous session ID and hashed device/IP fingerprint support moderation; chat messages are logged securely for 30 days. Your optional chat nickname is the only name shown to strangers.

  • Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, sexual content, and bullying are not allowed.
  • Never share your phone number, home address, social handle, password, or other private details.
  • Chats are stored securely so administrators can review safety reports. Each report includes up to 20 recent messages.
  • Use Report or Block if a conversation makes you uncomfortable. Three reports in 24 hours can trigger an automatic 24-hour suspension.
  • This is not an emergency service. For immediate help, contact the school counselor or the crisis resource in the footer.
